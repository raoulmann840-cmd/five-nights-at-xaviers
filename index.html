How to Play

You are Raoul Mann, locked in Classroom 13 overnight. Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM.

A / ←Run to the Hall Door (classroom door to the hallway)
D / →Run to the Storage Door (dark storage closet)
W / ↑Check the Supply Locker
S / ↓Turn around to the desks & teacher's podium · or go back
Hold Mouse / F / ShiftFlashlight beam (aim with the mouse). Battery is limited.
Hold Space / Right-clickHold a door or the locker shut
Esc · H · MPause · control hints · mute

The Three

  • Steven Pivac — the hallway. Footsteps echo down the corridor. Far away? Hit him with the beam and he backs off. Breathing right outside the door? Light OFF — hold the door until he walks away.
  • Xavier — the storage closet. Boxes shift; he whispers. Same rules, less patience. He also sends Little Xaviers onto the desks behind you — turn around and flash them away before three gather, or he rises behind the podium.
  • Zynovan Snusvac — the supply locker. Listen for metal creaks, the latch clicking, a snus tin rattling. Check it and hold it shut. Fully out? Hold it until the banging stops.

There are no on-screen warnings. Stand at a door with the light OFF to listen — sound is your only clue. Audio is 3D (HRTF): wear headphones.

Asset Guide

Everything you see is pre-rendered in 3D by the game at load time (Three.js, real shadows, procedural textures) and then lit live by a per-pixel flashlight shader (PixiJS). Your own files plug into that pipeline:

  • Faces (face.png) are wrapped onto the animatronic's 3D skull, shaded and shadowed with the body, and lit by your flashlight.
  • Sprites/backgrounds are color-graded and given generated normal maps so the beam rakes across them.
  1. In your GitHub repo, open the folder (e.g. assets/villains/xavier/).
  2. Add file → Upload files, drag the image in, rename it exactly as listed below.
  3. Commit. Netlify redeploys automatically — refresh the game.

Tips: face.png = front-facing headshot, face centred, eyes about 40% from the top. Jumpscare frames play in order (stage 1 = far, last = face in your face). Classroom backgrounds should be 16:9 renders from your own 3D software for a true FNAF look; the game scales everything to the player's screen.
