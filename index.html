How to Play

You are Raoul Mann. Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM in your bedroom. Three of them are coming.

A / ←Run to the Left Door
D / →Run to the Right Door
W / ↑Check the Closet
S / ↓Turn around to the Bed · or go back to the room
Hold Mouse / F / ShiftFlashlight (aim with the mouse). Battery is limited!
Hold Space / Right-clickHold a door or the closet shut
Esc · H · MPause · Toggle hints · Mute

The Three

  • Steven Pivac — Left hall. Heavy footsteps. Far away? Flash him and he retreats. Heavy breathing at the door? Do NOT use the light — hold the door until he leaves.
  • Xavier — Right hall. Fast, and he whispers. Same rules as Steven, but less patient. He also sends Little Xaviers onto your bed: turn around and flash them away before three gather… or he comes for you.
  • Zynovan Snusvac — The closet. Listen for a rattling snus tin. Check the closet and hold it shut to push him back. If he's fully out, hold the closet closed until the banging stops.

Tip: stand at a door with the light OFF to listen. Headphones help — sounds are panned left/right.

Asset Guide — add your friends' faces

The game runs out-of-the-box with vector monsters. Drop real photos into the repository's /assets/ folders and they are automatically color-graded (desaturated, high contrast, film grain, dark vignette, feathered edges) so they melt into the darkness.

  1. Open your GitHub repo → navigate into the folder (e.g. assets/villains/xavier/).
  2. Click Add file → Upload files and drag the photo in.
  3. Rename it to the exact file name below (lowercase). .png, .jpg, .jpeg or .webp all work.
  4. Commit. Netlify redeploys automatically; refresh the game.
assets/
├── player/
│   └── raoul.png         ← Raoul Mann headshot (menu & win screen)
└── villains/
    ├── xavier/
    │   ├── normal.png    ← headshot: becomes the animatronic's face
    │   ├── jumpscare.png ← (optional) full-screen scream photo
    │   └── hallway.png   ← (optional) full-body photo seen down the hall
    ├── steven/   (same three files)
    └── zynovan/  (same three files)

Photo tips: front-facing headshots, face centred, roughly square, ≤ 2 MB. For jumpscare.png a wide-open-mouth "scream" face is ideal. If jumpscare.png is missing the game zooms the normal.png face on the robot body instead.
